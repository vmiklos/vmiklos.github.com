Better rendering of track changes and numbering portions in Collabora Online Writer

Posted on: Fri 06 November 2026

Estimated read time: 2 minutes

Numbering portions are the special creatures that are automatically generated at the start of a paragraph for Arabic numbering or bullets. They are there before the paragraph even starts, and now the track changes rendering for them is better.

This post is meant to show how this looked in the past and what we improved recently.

Motivation¶

Users expect that if they make a text selection in a Writer document and track changes is on and they delete the content, then that content will be rendered as crossed out.
