LibreOffice Writer had an old limitation we inherited from OpenOffice.org
times: if a document started with a table, it wasn't possible to select the
whole document text. There were various bugreports for this problem:
[one](https://bugs.freedesktop.org/show_bug.cgi?id=37606) from more than 2 years
ago, an [other](https://issues.apache.org/ooo/show_bug.cgi?id=7747) from 2002 (!)
-- and I'm happy to post screenshots about how I improved this in LibreOffice
4.2 (before and after):

[![Old](https://lh6.googleusercontent.com/-MnQseLEsI8E/Ujxtf6Omt2I/AAAAAAAADac/JngDQwr3eH8/s400/old.png)](https://lh6.googleusercontent.com/-MnQseLEsI8E/Ujxtf6Omt2I/AAAAAAAADac/JngDQwr3eH8/s1000/old.png)

[![New](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/-ZvFwofXGkWM/Ujxtf6TBrlI/AAAAAAAADag/HAutIx5NBBY/s400/new.png)](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/-ZvFwofXGkWM/Ujxtf6TBrlI/AAAAAAAADag/HAutIx5NBBY/s1000/new.png)


If you want to try this out yourself, get a
[daily build](http://dev-builds.libreoffice.org/daily/) and play with it. :-)
