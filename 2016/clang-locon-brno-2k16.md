The last week I gave a
[Using
clang-based tools beyond loplugin](http://conference.libreoffice.org/2016/the-program/sept-9th-friday/) lightning talk at LibreOffice conference
2016, on the last day. Click on the image to see all the slides.

If you're a vim or emacs user and you work with `C++11` code, you probably
want to have a look at
[clang-rename](http://clang.llvm.org/extra/clang-rename.html),
[include-fixer](http://clang.llvm.org/extra/include-fixer.html) and some editor
plugin exposing the power of libclang (like
[YouCompleteMe](https://valloric.github.io/YouCompleteMe/) or
[libclang-vim](https://github.com/libclang-vim/libclang-vim)), sometimes these
are really helpful.
