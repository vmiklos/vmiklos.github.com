On Saturday I gave a talk at the [Open IT 2015](http://openit.eventbrite.com/)
conference about the new features of LibreOffice 4.3 and 4.4.  My uploaded
slides are available
[here](https://speakerdeck.com/vmiklos/4-dot-4-es-kiadasban).

Thanks [Óbuda University](https://www.uni-obuda.hu/en) for hosting us, it was a
great event! Other than talking to the usual suspects like
[Tamás Zolnai](http://zolnaitamas.blogspot.com/) or
[Gábor Kelemen](https://plus.google.com/117194932353487735021/posts), I enjoyed
two OpenStreetMap talks: it was extermely cool to hear that finally the
[turistautak.hu](http://turistautak.hu/) community
[changed
their license](http://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/WikiProject_Hungary/Import%C3%A1l%C3%A1s/turistautak.hu) in February so that all their free maps can be imported to
OpenStreetMap -- finally one pointless fight ends.
