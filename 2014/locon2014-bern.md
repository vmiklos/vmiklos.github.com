This year's LibreOffice conference was held in Bern, Switzerland. Links to my slides:

- [Inconsistencies Fixed in Writer](https://speakerdeck.com/vmiklos/inconsistencies-fixed-in-writer)
- [Proudly breaking your toolchain](https://speakerdeck.com/vmiklos/proudly-breaking-your-toolchain)

During the sessions I also had some time to hack on the followings:

- RTF export: added support for custom wrap polygon of Writer pictures
- fixed [fdo#82067](https://bugs.freedesktop.org/show_bug.cgi?id=82067)
  FILEOPEN: RTF images not in correct position
- fixed [fdo#82078](https://bugs.freedesktop.org/show_bug.cgi?id=82078)
  FILEOPEN: RTF bold text spilling over to non-bold text
- fixed [abi#10039](http://bugzilla.abisource.com/show_bug.cgi?id=10039) crasher
  on RTF export of page-anchored pictures
