I've arrived home yesterday from Brussels where I
[presented](https://fosdem.org/2014/schedule/event/interopgrabbag_in_libreoffice_writer/)
at FOSDEM 2014, in the
[Open document
editors devroom](https://fosdem.org/2014/schedule/track/open_document_editors/).

We also had a
[Hackfest](https://wiki.documentfoundation.org/Hackfest/FOSDEM2014), kindly
hosted by [Betacowork](http://www.betacowork.com/) on Monday and Tuesday.

Here are a few talks I enjoyed, not counting the LibreOffice ones:

- [Simplifying
  reuse with metadata support in ODF and plugin APIs](https://fosdem.org/2014/schedule/event/simplifying_reuse_with_metadata_support_in_odf_and_plugin_apis/) -- finally a simple way
  to copy&paste images from Firefox to LibreOffice with source attribution.
- [Combining the power of
  Valgrind and GDB](https://fosdem.org/2014/schedule/event/valgrind_gdb/) -- useful to know about this possibility
- [Two uses
  cases for the clang C++ parser: Online Code Browser and Qt moc Replacement.](https://fosdem.org/2014/schedule/event/usecasesclangcppparser/)
  -- maybe the first could replace our crappy Java-based opengrok? We'll see.

I was also happy to meet Jacobo, Matus, Tim and Tomaž finally personally. :-)
