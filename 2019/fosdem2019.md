The next step in the recent [SmartArt story]({filename}/2019/smartart-improvements-3.md) is my
[Improving SmartArt import in Impress](https://fosdem.org/2019/schedule/event/improving_smartart_import_in_libreoffice_impress/)

room was a bit far away from the popular places, but the livestream worked out nicely.

[hackfest](https://wiki.documentfoundation.org/Events/2019/FOSDEM#Hackfest) before the conference, I
looked at [RTF export of rotated Writer pictures](https://bugs.documentfoundation.org/show_bug.cgi?id=116371#c20).
