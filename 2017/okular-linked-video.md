Title: okular now supports online videos in PDF files
Slug: okular-linked-video
Category: okular
Tags: en
Date: 2017-02-27T09:53:06Z

I rarely write about work did by others, but given that in the
[previous post]({filename}/2017/pdf-video-export.md) I mentioned that linked videos (when they are a
http:// URL) are not working, I had a very positive experience which is worth noting. :-)

I got a private mail nagging me to file a [bug](https://bugs.kde.org/show_bug.cgi?id=376574) and
just 3 days later it got fixed!

Thanks to both Oliver for the nagging and to Albert for the actual fix.
