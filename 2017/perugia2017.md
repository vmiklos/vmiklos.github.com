Last weekend I attended the LibreOffice [Perugia](https://wiki.documentfoundation.org/Hackfest/Perugia2017)
HackFest 2017, with the primary goal of mentoring students (together with
[Eike](http://erack.org/blog/) and Christian): provided they manage to contribute at least one
non-trivial easy hack, they get university credits for their work.

I worked with [Arianna](https://gerrit.libreoffice.org/38160),
[Claudio](https://gerrit.libreoffice.org/38159), [Francesco](https://gerrit.libreoffice.org/38161)
and [Gian](https://gerrit.libreoffice.org/38134), all of them managed to achieve something by the
end of the third day.

When I was not helping others, I also fixed a few bugs:

- [tdf#107976](https://bugs.documentfoundation.org/show_bug.cgi?id=107976) sw: let a view handle multiple transferables
- [tdf#107837](https://bugs.documentfoundation.org/show_bug.cgi?id=107837) DOCX export: fix balanced multi-col section at doc end
- [tdf#107684](https://bugs.documentfoundation.org/show_bug.cgi?id=107684) DOCX export: fix duplicated <w:outlineLvl> element for styles
- [tdf#106950](https://bugs.documentfoundation.org/show_bug.cgi?id=106950) sw: support CharShadingValue property on paragraph styles

Some photos I took during the event are [available](https://www.flickr.com/photos/vmiklos/albums/72157681387507534).

Thanks the organizers for the great event, also kudos to [Collabora](https://www.collaboraoffice.com/),
Red Hat and TDF for allowing mentors to come! :-)
