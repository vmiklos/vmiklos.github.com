Did you ever notice the [CSS Validator](http://jigsaw.w3.org/css-validator/) does not support HTTPS? I sent two patches ([ant dependencies fix](http://dev.w3.org/cvsweb/2002/css-validator/build.xml#rev1.17), [SSL Host verifier fix](http://dev.w3.org/cvsweb/2002/css-validator/org/w3c/css/css/DocumentParser.java#rev1.5)) to its devel list a while ago and they are now in CVS, hopefully they will soon upgrade their service as well. :)
