Aaand this time, as a very special gift, ppc packages are [available](http://ftp.frugalware.org/pub/other/people/vmiklos/ooo33/frugalware-ppc/) as well! :)

I just built it, but Bouleetbil was kind enough to provide a [screenshot](http://dors.frugalware.org/libreofficeppc.png) when he tested it.
