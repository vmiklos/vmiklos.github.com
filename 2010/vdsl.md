Time to say bye-bye to adsl!

The optical fiber is now only [260 meters](http://maps.google.com/maps?f=d&source=s_d&saddr=1124+Budapest,+N%C3%A9metv%C3%B6lgyi+Way+55,+Hungary&daddr=1124+Budapest,+Kiss+J%C3%A1nos+Alt%C3%A1bornagy+Way+57,+Hungary&hl=en&geocode=Feau1AIdfhkiASkV2naYg95BRzG9tgLExaGY7Q%3B&mra=ls&sll=47.492935,19.01343&sspn=0.001783,0.004823&ie=UTF8&z=18) away and that means it's possible to order vDSL here.

That means that means that the average download speed is now about 14MBit/s while it was about 5MBit/s with adsl. :)
